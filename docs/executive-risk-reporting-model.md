# 📊 Executive Cyber Risk Reporting Model

## 📌 Purpose

This document demonstrates a governance-focused executive reporting model used to communicate cybersecurity risks, operational issues, and security posture updates to leadership stakeholders.

The objective is to support:
- Executive visibility
- Risk-informed decision-making
- Governance accountability
- Operational transparency
- Regulatory readiness

---

# 🎯 Governance Objectives

- Translate technical risk into business impact
- Improve leadership awareness
- Support governance oversight
- Enable strategic decision-making
- Improve accountability tracking

---

# 👥 Executive Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Reporting Focus |
|---|---|
| Executive Leadership | Enterprise cyber risk posture |
| CIO / CISO | Operational security maturity |
| Compliance Leadership | Regulatory exposure |
| Risk Committees | Residual risk tracking |
| IT Leadership | Operational weaknesses |
| Audit Teams | Control effectiveness |

---

# 📈 Executive Reporting Categories

## Security Operations
- Incident trends
- Mean Time to Detect (MTTD)
- Mean Time to Respond (MTTR)
- Detection coverage
- SIEM monitoring maturity

---

## Risk Governance
- Open high-risk findings
- Residual risk exposure
- Control effectiveness gaps
- Risk treatment status
- Governance escalation metrics

---

## Compliance & Audit
- Audit findings status
- Policy review completion
- Evidence collection tracking
- Regulatory issue management

---

## Operational Resilience
- Recovery readiness
- Escalation efficiency
- Incident communication effectiveness
- Business continuity alignment
