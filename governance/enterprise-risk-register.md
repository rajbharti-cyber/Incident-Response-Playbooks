# 📋 Enterprise Cyber Risk Register

## 📌 Purpose

This document demonstrates a governance-oriented cyber risk register used to identify, assess, prioritize, and manage cybersecurity risks within an enterprise environment.

The objective is to support:
- Risk visibility
- Governance oversight
- Audit readiness
- Control accountability
- Risk-informed decision-making

---

# 🎯 Governance Objectives

- Identify critical cyber risks
- Evaluate inherent and residual risk
- Assign risk ownership
- Support remediation tracking
- Improve governance accountability

---

# 🔍 Risk Rating Methodology

| Rating | Description |
|---|---|
| Critical | Severe operational or regulatory impact |
| High | Significant business disruption |
| Medium | Moderate operational impact |
| Low | Limited business impact |
