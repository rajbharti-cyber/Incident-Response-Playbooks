# 📢 Incident Escalation & Communication Workflow

## 📌 Purpose

This document defines the governance-oriented communication and escalation workflow for information security incidents.

The objective is to ensure:
- Timely incident escalation
- Clear stakeholder communication
- Consistent governance oversight
- Regulatory coordination where required
- Effective operational response

---

# 🎯 Escalation Objectives

- Reduce communication delays
- Improve coordination during incidents
- Support executive visibility
- Maintain accountability
- Enhance operational resilience

---

# 🚨 Escalation Workflow

## 1️⃣ Incident Detection
Security incidents may be identified through:
- SIEM alerts
- Monitoring tools
- User reports
- Threat intelligence
- Audit findings
- Third-party notifications

---

## 2️⃣ Initial Assessment
The security or incident response team should:
- Validate the incident
- Assess potential impact
- Determine severity classification
- Initiate incident documentation

---

## 3️⃣ Escalation Decision
Incidents should be escalated based on:
- Business impact
- Regulatory implications
- Data sensitivity
- Operational disruption
- Customer exposure

---

## 4️⃣ Stakeholder Notification

| Stakeholder | Notification Purpose |
|---|---|
| Security Team | Technical response coordination |
| IT Operations | Recovery and remediation |
| Leadership | Business impact awareness |
| Compliance Team | Regulatory assessment |
| Legal Team | Legal and contractual review |
| HR Team | Insider-related incidents |

---

## 5️⃣ Executive Communication
Critical incidents should include:
- Executive status updates
- Risk summaries
- Recovery progress
- Business impact reporting

---

## 6️⃣ External Communication
Where required:
- Customers may be notified
- Regulatory bodies may be informed
- Third-party vendors may be engaged

All external communication should be coordinated through approved governance channels.

---

# 📋 Escalation Severity Guidance

| Severity | Escalation Requirement |
|---|---|
| Critical | Immediate executive escalation |
| High | Management escalation within 1 hour |
| Medium | Operational escalation during business hours |
| Low | Standard documentation and tracking |

---

# 👥 Governance Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Incident Manager | Escalation coordination |
| Security Team | Technical communication |
| Leadership | Business decision-making |
| Compliance Team | Regulatory coordination |
| Legal Team | External communication review |
